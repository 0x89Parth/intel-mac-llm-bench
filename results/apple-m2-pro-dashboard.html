Method and source reports

PP512 processes a 512-token prompt. TG128 generates 128 tokens. Both models use Q4_K_M weights. The paired runs were warmed up, used six threads, and ran tuned first, then stock on 2026-11-18 at 21:33 UTC. Stock used batch 2,048 and microbatch 512. Tuned used 256 for both. The initial sweep selected tuned batch 256 from the two explicit batch sizes based on prompt throughput.

With one pair per model at a fixed run order, the small decoding differences need another check to establish repeatability.
